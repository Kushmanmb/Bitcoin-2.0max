# Bitcoin 2.0max Manifesto

Bitcoin 2.0max exists to push peer-to-peer money toward its original ideal:
open, borderless, censorship-resistant value transfer that remains accessible
to ordinary people.

## What We Believe

- **Freedom first:** money should be controlled by users, not gatekeepers.
- **Performance matters:** faster blocks and larger throughput are necessary for
  real-world adoption.
- **Interoperability wins:** open standards (like Electrum) reduce lock-in and
  strengthen the ecosystem.
- **Resilience over hype:** reliability, security, and predictable operation are
  more important than narratives.
- **Open development:** transparent code, review, and roadmap decisions build
  long-term trust.

## Our Commitments

- Build practical infrastructure that can run on commodity hardware.
- Keep the stack modular so wallets and services can integrate easily.
- Prioritize clear documentation and reproducible builds.
- Evolve carefully: improve performance without abandoning Bitcoin’s core
  principles of verification and decentralization.
- Welcome contributors who share these goals and challenge ideas in good faith.

## Call to Builders

If you believe sound money should also be fast, scalable, and open, build with
us. Run a node, review code, propose improvements, and help make Bitcoin 2.0max
useful for the next generation of global users.
